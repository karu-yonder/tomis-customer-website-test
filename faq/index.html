FAQ

Short answers. The chat button can take a question that is not on this list.

How old do I have to be?

16 or older for a tandem, with a parent or guardian at check-in if you are under 18.

Is there a weight limit?

100kg in the clothes you will jump in. Above that, ask before you book so we can check the aircraft for that day.

What if the weather turns?

We move you to the next load or the next day. A weather hold is not a cancellation fee.

Can I jump with glasses?

Yes. We tape them or give you a strap. Contact lenses are fine.
