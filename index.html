How a jump works

1. Check in Paperwork, a harness, and a short brief on the landing.
2. The ride up About 20 minutes to altitude, with the door open for the view.
3. Freefall Your instructor flies the jump. You look at the coast.
4. Beach landing A canopy ride down, then photos if you added a media package.
