Gift vouchers

A voucher holds a tandem at one drop zone for 12 months. The person who receives it picks the day.

  • Choose Byron Bay, Cairns, or Noosa when you buy it.
  • Media packages can be added later. They are not on the voucher.
  • Vouchers on this demo site are not issued and cannot be redeemed.

Voucher amounts follow the prices page
