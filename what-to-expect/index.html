Home / What to expect

Your first tandem

You do not need to have jumped before. The instructor wears the parachute. You wear a harness that clips to theirs.

Before you leave home

  • Eat a normal meal. An empty stomach is worse than a small breakfast.
  • Leave loose jewellery and hats in the car.
  • Bring closed shoes. Thongs stay on the ground.

In the air

You sit on the floor of the aircraft for the climb. At altitude you shuffle to the door, the instructor counts you out, and the freefall starts. After the canopy opens, the rest of the ride is quiet enough to talk.
