Byron Bay

Tandem skydives at our Byron Bay drop zone.

Tandem jumps from 10,000 and 15,000 feet. You are attached to an instructor for the whole jump. The freefall lasts about 30 to 60 seconds, then the canopy ride back to the beach is several minutes.

On the day

  • Arrive 45 minutes before your load.
  • Weight limit 100kg for a standard tandem. Ask in chat if you are close to the limit.
  • Wear sneakers and clothes you can move in. We provide the jump suit.

What you see

The lighthouse sits on the most easterly point. On a clear load you can pick out the bay, the hinterland, and the beach you will land on.
